# Northwind Robotics Handbook

Everything here applies to full-time employees unless a section says otherwise. If a rule is unclear, ask the People team before you act on it.

## VPN and remote access

Connect to the corporate VPN before you open any internal tool from outside the office. A VPN session expires after 12 hours, and you sign in again with your hardware key. If the VPN client fails to connect three times in a row, restart your laptop before you open a ticket. Contractors keep VPN access only until their contract ends.

## Passwords and hardware keys

Passwords must be at least 16 characters long. Passwords are rotated every 180 days. Every employee receives two hardware keys and should keep the spare one at home. Report a lost hardware key to the security desk within 24 hours. The password manager is the only approved place to store credentials.

## Laptops and equipment

Laptops are replaced every 3 years. A laptop screen locks automatically after 5 minutes without input. Accidental damage to a laptop is covered once per year. When you leave the company, return all equipment within 10 business days.

## Expenses

Submit an expense report within 30 days of the purchase. Meals while traveling are reimbursed up to 60 dollars per day. Any purchase above 500 dollars needs written approval from your manager before you buy. Approved expenses are paid out with the next payroll run.

## Paid time off

Full-time employees accrue 1.5 days of paid time off per month. Up to 5 unused days of paid time off carry over into the next year. Request time off at least 2 weeks in advance when you will be away for more than 3 days. Contractors do not accrue paid time off.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Parental leave has to start within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. A one-time home office stipend of 800 dollars is available after 90 days of employment. Working from another country for more than 14 days needs approval from the People team.

## On-call

An engineer who is on call must acknowledge a page within 15 minutes. On-call shifts last one week and change hands every Monday at 10:00. Each on-call week earns one extra day off.

## Security incidents

Report a suspected phishing email with the Report button and do not forward it. A lost or stolen laptop must be reported within 1 hour so that it can be wiped remotely. The security desk is staffed around the clock at extension 7700.

## Travel

Book flights at least 21 days before departure. Economy class is the standard for flights shorter than 6 hours. Hotel stays are reimbursed up to 220 dollars per night.

## Learning budget

Each employee has a learning budget of 1,500 dollars per year. Attending a conference needs approval at least 6 weeks in advance. Unused learning budget does not carry over.

## Health and wellness

The company pays 100 percent of the health insurance premium for employees. For dependents, the company pays 50 percent of the health insurance premium. A wellness allowance of 40 dollars per month covers gym memberships and fitness classes.

## Performance reviews

Performance reviews happen twice a year, in March and September. Salary changes take effect on the first of April. Your manager shares written feedback at least 3 days before the review meeting.

## Office access

Office badges are deactivated after 30 days without use. Guests must be signed in at the front desk and wear a visitor badge. The office is open to badge holders from 6:00 to 22:00 on weekdays.
